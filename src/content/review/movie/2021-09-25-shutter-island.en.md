Spoilers ahead

I had the plot of Shutter Island spectacularly wrong in my head.

I was expecting some Disney film about life inside a dome..? Maybe watching it in that state made it even better.

I didn't even know it was a film with a twist, so at the end I got blindsided right alongside Leo.

## Plot
Up to the twist it's just a mystery about finding a prisoner who vanished from the facility. But Leo can never find that prisoner.

I completely missed all the seeded clues while watching, and the ending — realising, as he fires the gun, that this entire process was part of the treatment, and the way Leo's face collapses in that moment — was so sad.

And the line he quietly hands the doctor while pretending the treatment failed: "Which would be worse — to live as a monster, or to die as a good man?" If you put yourself in Leo's place you can't begin to measure what's behind that.

His wife lost her mind and killed their children, he killed her, and to forget it he split off a self and went mad — and to be treated he first has to recognise and accept that reality. I don't think you could call him a monster for going on living as he was, but that would have been unbearable for him. Anyway.. the clue-breakdown videos I looked up afterwards were great fun too.
