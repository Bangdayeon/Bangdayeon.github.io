I once tried to watch The Dark Knight and switched it off early because I wasn't enjoying it. Maybe I just wasn't in the mood that day.. Anyway, this is the first DC hero film I've actually watched through. So I can't really say how its mood differs from other DC films.

For a start the atmosphere is deeply bleak. Batman is also extremely serious, calm and taciturn.

Gotham, the city Batman lives in, rains constantly and has given up on opening out or developing, with every kind of criminal gathered there. The upper class is riddled with corruption. Rather than live in a place like that, starting over from nothing in another city looks like it has more of a future. And Batman is out there alone trying to revive it, catching villains with money-drenched gear and his fists.

There's more deduction and investigation than action, and a lot of Gotham's gloom.

It feels similar to Joker and yet different. As I understand it, the Joaquin Phoenix Joker I watched before is a different continuity from DC's Joker. DC's Joker is simply a madman, whereas Joaquin's carries the narrative of *becoming* the Joker. Both are dark and clammy. Joker's lead is a dancing madman while Batman's is grave and heavy, and I think that's where the moods fork further apart. What they share is that, stripped of the villain/hero role, as ordinary people both are constantly anxious and in pain. Honestly it's been a while since I saw Joker so I don't remember it properly heh... I should watch it again soon!

It did drag a little, but I enjoyed it. Also Robert Pattinson is genuinely handsome..
