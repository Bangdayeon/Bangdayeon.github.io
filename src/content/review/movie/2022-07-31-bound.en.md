## Plot

Corky, just out of prison and working as a repairwoman at a hotel, and Violet, who lives with Caesar, a mob money-launderer, meet in the hotel lift, fall for each other at first sight, and plan to steal the money Caesar has just laundered.

Corky makes the plan believing Caesar is a coward. But Caesar turns out to be far more unhinged than expected.

Anyway, in love, the two of them plan to take Caesar's money.

Caesar and some other mobsters cut off a man's finger and bring back two million dollars. The money is soaked in blood, so Caesar washes it, dries it, irons it, sorts it and puts it in a bag.

The plan is this.

The mob boss coming to collect the money will visit Caesar and Violet's room. Violet is supposed to serve the boss's favourite drink, so she will break it as if by accident and go out to buy more — and at that moment Corky enters the room, takes the money and leaves newspaper in its place.

When Corky returns she will lie that she saw the boss's son, who had gone to fetch the boss, leaving. So Caesar, anxious that the son has taken the money, will open the bag and find newspaper, and out of fear that the boss and the son are setting him up to be killed, Caesar will run. In the meantime Violet escapes too and takes off with Corky and the money.

But it all goes to plan until Caesar, more unhinged than expected, does not run. He holds out, and the boss and the son arrive. The boss checks the bag. The money is of course gone. Caesar points a gun at the son demanding to know where the money is, and the son of course has no idea. Caesar kills the boss, the son and two bodyguards in an instant. (Oh my god....) A mere money-launderer killed a mob boss.

Police arrive in the middle of that, and Caesar quickly smooths it over and roughly hides the bodies in the bathroom. He even phones the other mobsters to buy time, saying the boss and the son have not turned up.

Then, while two more frightening people — someone above the boss? or a contact? — are on their way, knowing nothing and wondering why the others have not come, foolishly, so foolishly, Violet and Corky are on the phone in the room right next door and get caught by Caesar. Both of them. Caesar goes to cut off Violet's finger, so Corky blurts out that the money is inside the paint in the next room, and Caesar points the gun at Corky. But Corky says one line — "you can't kill me. I might be lying" — and Caesar, thrown, goes off to check.

The moment he finds the bag, the two mobsters arrive. He manages to fool them and send them off, and Violet bolts. Caesar chases her, and Violet is clever: she runs down the stairs at full pelt, which had me wondering what she was doing, and then takes the lift back up from the ground floor. Caesar chases her down and then has to run all the way back up the stairs..... And on the way up in the lift Violet phones those two mobsters, acting terrified, saying Caesar made her do it and please come quickly, then rides calmly up.

Meanwhile Corky cuts her ropes with the finger-cutting knife and escapes. But she is caught again in the next room and is about to be killed by Caesar, when Violet points a gun at him.

Caesar, with the most relaxed expression, says "you can't kill me. If you could, you'd have done it long ago" — and Violet shoots him, cleanly and decisively. Many times. Caesar collapses onto the white paint he had poured over the floor looking for the money, and the blood spreads through it. Quite a scene....

Then Violet tells the two mobsters she is too frightened to carry on and wants out, and gets away with Corky.

## Afterwards

I watched it because the poster is so cool.

The overall mood was wonderful. A sort of classic action-noir feel?

And Corky's mouth is so striking. Like Aiki with the mouth cut a little wider. She looks so cool.

It also feels dense. Almost all of it happens in two hotel rooms, and in that neither-large-nor-small space, whenever Caesar is in trouble his mind suddenly starts spinning fast and his luck improves, so tense situations keep arriving. Police using the bathroom where the bodies are hidden, treading on the carpet where the blood is hidden, the two mobsters nearly getting the money bag. I was completely absorbed.

The ending, driving off in a brand-new red car, was cool too.
