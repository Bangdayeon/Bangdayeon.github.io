So *that* was the origin of the meme — Strange, personality problems and all, going to meet Dormammu prepared to sacrifice his whole life for the people of the world. Though the head of that religion? being a bald foreigner was a bit out of nowhere.

The scene and the line when the Ancient One dies were lovely.

> [!quote]
> "We don't get to choose our time. Death is what gives life meaning. To know your days are numbered and your time is short. You'd think after all this time I'd be ready. But look at me. Stretching one moment out into a thousand, just so that I can watch the snow."

Death makes life. Even eternal life might not be possible without the concept of death. Things change as time passes, so life exists — and the fact that there is change may mean that death sits at the end of it.
