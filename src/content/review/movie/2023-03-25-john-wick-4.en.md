I haven't seen any of the previous films, so I don't really know John Wick
And it's been over a month, and the film was long, and I'm not sure what it even was, so I don't remember it well. On top of that I watched it in the original with no subtitles..

## Plot with spoilers

Something seems to have happened to the hotel John Wick relied on. He seems to have been on the run from the Marquis de Gramont's people.

With that hotel gone, John Wick takes refuge in another assassins' hotel in Japan. There, the hotel's people fight those who came for him and many die (poor them..) And Caine, a blind but highly skilled assassin, comes at Gramont's threatening request and fights John Wick. A bounty hunter also appears here and there.

Nothing gets settled, so the three of them go to see some club owner. They play cards, it doesn't go the way he wants, and John Wick kills the club owner. Then, to play a final game for his freedom, John Wick has to climb a long flight of stairs. He works his way up alone, shooting, and just before the top he takes a hit and tumbles all the way back down. It felt like a two- or three-minute fall.

Down at the bottom John Wick, Caine and the bounty hunter (and his dog) gather, and the three of them go up together.

For the final game John Wick and Caine each fire one shot at the same time.

It's best of three, and at the end John Wick pretends to have taken a fatal hit, so Gramont goes "I'll finish him myself!", takes Caine's gun, walks up close to John Wick, and is shot dead by him.

John Wick staggers a little way down, looks at a photo of his late wife on the steps, collapses, and the film ends on a shot of his grave.

## Afterwards

I knew John Wick was famous but had never seen it, and the route by which I ended up watching it was:

followed a friend to Canada on a working holiday → friend's cousin said John Wick 4 was opening and suggested we go → went

That's how it happened.. So the night before I watched a bit of the first one while dozing off.

In the first one I thought, ah, John Wick lost his dog and his car so he's going to kill that mob family's son! — and then, having killed the son, John Wick still has no car and no dog and even loses the friend who helped him. (What?)

Anyway, with roughly that much knowledge I saw John Wick 4 in a cinema on 25 March, its Canadian release date.

The action was fun and satisfying, but... it honestly wasn't to my taste.

The film ran nearly three hours, and I don't understand why the fairly long Japanese assassins' hotel section was in it.

John Wick just stops by the hotel, the mob raid it because of that, and John Wick goes "sorry..." and simply leaves. (This might just be me not following the film properly.)

Everyone else is firing guns while the daughter alone shoots arrows from about five paces away, which is odd, and once she's out of arrows she goes around whacking people with the bow, which is odd, and after fighting hard in what looks like a ninja outfit she ends up letting her guard down and taking a bullet to the stomach.

John Wick fights Caine in a Japanese art and weapons exhibition space, loses his gun midway, farms a pair of nunchaku from beside him and shows off swinging them around his body before running off.

Caine parts from John Wick and fights the owner who was supporting his daughter; the owner dies and the daughter cries "Father!" before Caine lets her go and she vanishes on a train.. The daughter's outfit and fighting style and John Wick's nunchaku sequence made me cringe.

John Wick and Caine and the bounty hunter and the club owner playing cards is funny too,,,, and the club crowd dance extremely hard. It didn't look like club dancing at all.... John Wick is fighting someone right next to them and they don't even glance over, and only when the club owner appears do they all go o0o and stare.

The stairs scene was genuinely bizarre.... I was watching thinking, lots of action here, and then the tumble was too much... He even stops partway down, then pushes off the ground with his palm and rolls himself the rest of the way to the bottom. He falls for so long that everyone in the cinema burst out laughing (what even was that)

Caine and the bounty hunter suddenly appear and help John Wick, and they all go up for the final game, and John Wick kills Gramont and finally wins his freedom (though he seems to die immediately after..) — and the bounty hunter, who has no relationship with either of them, watches the game between Caine and John Wick with tears welling up (why did you come up here...)

So..... I did enjoy it, but it's a film I never quite worked out~~

One star is for the action scenes and one is for the Gramont fashion show (Bill Skarsgård).
