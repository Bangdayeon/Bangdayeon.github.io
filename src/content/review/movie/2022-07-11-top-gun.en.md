## Plot

Maverick and Goose enter Top Gun as trainees.

The night before the first session, Maverick and Goose try and fail to chat up a woman at a bar, and the next day she turns up as their instructor.

They train hard, and then the over-confident Maverick does as he pleases again, gets caught in a sudden natural phenomenon and ends up in danger. Maverick and Goose, in the same aircraft, realise they're in trouble and eject — but Goose catches on the way out from the back seat and dies. Maverick isn't disciplined because it was an unpredictable natural event, but he blames himself, believing Goose wouldn't have died if he hadn't rushed the attack, and his confidence collapses.

He gets through it somehow, though, turns up at graduation and graduates safely.

The graduates all board some ship, the situation goes wrong, and they end up having to fight enemy fighters.

When Iceman is in danger, Maverick thankfully overcomes his trauma, flies at his best, wins through and comes home.

And he throws Goose's dog tags into the sea.

## Afterwards

I did enjoy it, but personally Maverick was more fun. I think a lot of that is the difference in the quality of the jet sequences.

Still, the mood of the film and the OST and the pre-flight preparation scenes were good. Honestly I was a little sleepy.

And Tom Cruise is handsome to a remarkable degree.
