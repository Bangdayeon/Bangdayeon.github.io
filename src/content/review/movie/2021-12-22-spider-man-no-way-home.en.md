Spoilers ahead

Beat my chest internally from start to finish.
Early on: the citizens have lost it..
Early-mid: Peter has lost it..
Late: brief rest
Ending: Marvel has lost it!!

Spider-Man really is the most pitiable. "Your friendly neighbourhood" is far too heavy a title for a gentle, broke teenage spider hero in this harsh world.

Being forgotten by everyone.. how can anyone be handed an ending like that...
A kid who's only seventeen gets bitten by the wrong spider one day and becomes a hero,, has no money,, has stared at MIT his whole life, and nobody looks after him.. giving it all up and calling it growing up is just too cruel.

Amazing Spider-Man finally catching a falling Zendaya was also unhinged.
How am I supposed to wait for the next Spider-Man ㅜ
