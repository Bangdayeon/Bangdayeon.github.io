I watched it even though it's a pile-up of genres I don't usually go for.

I hadn't planned to, but it came up while drinking with Hwa-hee and I rode the impulse. And I ended up enjoying it.

The characters were fun — Bubs being an idiot and yet ridiculously strong, Captain Jang being filthy but a righteous genius, Tiger Park looking like the most violent man alive while being the one who plays best with Kot-nim, Tae-ho's driving, cute Kot-nim, and the UTS founder who is clearly Not Right.

## 📝 Plot

Earth has gotten too filthy, so people now live out on space stations. James developed a planet into the paradise of UTS, and people are slowly migrating there.

The Victory crew are the thugs of the space-junk collector world. They just snatch whatever debris other people find.. and during one of those thug moves they get hold of an abandoned ship, where they find **Kot-nim, introduced on the news as a robot with a hydrogen bomb inside her**.

Money-hungry Tae-ho tries to cut a deal with the Black Foxes, who supposedly stole the bomb, but the UTS military barges in and it falls apart.

Then, talking to the Black Foxes, they learn that UTS was only sustainable as a paradise *because* of Kot-nim, and that the founder now plans to blow up both her and Earth — so the Victory crew fight to stop it.

## 🧙‍♂️ The Wizard of Oz
![The Wizard of Oz poster](https://i.namu.wiki/i/eejFDNYT4MAlo6Pr9ti2gkbS6n8pUndanI6iOtuvm-e9tdm4uTwvSFNq9tDh87SGACf-6eE0eMfUP0a50Qh5s65Q8c1kHEZdFa2SbKUP4ofJbq5YsT_3-xyPV3z0ocg0PfdIVWWGo97EN7oSR_nXgg.webp)
I only realised while writing the plot out that the characters are built on **The Wizard of Oz**.

- Kot-nim — Dorothy
- Bubs — the Tin Woodman
- Tiger Park — the Cowardly Lion

The rest.. no idea.. maybe there isn't one.

## 🫤 What disappointed me

Enjoying it is one thing, but the story and the characters felt underbuilt.

For a start every character is extremely one-dimensional, which is probably why none of them really grab you. James, the UTS founder, especially has zero depth and just behaves like a villain from a kids' cartoon. He swans around UTS in white clothes playing the nice guy, then suddenly cackles evilly. Chasing the bomb-laden Victory down *by himself* was ridiculous too 🙄

And I couldn't connect with Tae-ho at all.. I understand his situation, but personally he didn't work for me. He's the lead so he gets the most screen time, and I neither felt for him nor enjoyed the melodrama wedged in there.

## 👍 What I liked

But if you go in holding "this is a Wizard of Oz riff" in your head, it does read as a fairy tale where everyone overcomes their trauma or gets what they wanted and sends the child Kot-nim home — and through that lens I had fun with it.

And because the story is simple you can switch your brain off.

I also liked how well it worked Korean texture into SF. The translator device, the tomatoes, the braised beans, the flag painted on the Victory — none of that shows up in the SF I'd seen before.
