## Plot with spoilers

Hae-jun, an exemplary detective, takes on a case involving a man who fell to his death on a mountain, and after seeing the flat affect of the man's wife, Seo-rae, he puts her on the suspect list. As the investigation proceeds, he becomes interested in her.

Even after developing feelings, Hae-jun keeps suspecting Seo-rae as a detective — but her alibi is verified, so he can relax and take her off the list. Then he happens to learn that the alibi was meticulously constructed, and knowing that, he lets her go anyway because of his feelings, saying that he has been completely shattered by it — and he makes his decision to leave her.

Hae-jun, who had been living apart from his wife, moves to where she is in order to forget Seo-rae and continues working as a detective there. While investigating a death, he meets Seo-rae once again as the dead man's wife and a suspect. This time Hae-jun is certain she is the culprit and goes hunting for evidence, but this time she had only induced the killing.

In the process Seo-rae makes her own decision to leave, for Hae-jun's sake — and at the same time decides to become one of his unsolved cases. (His unsolved cases are pinned up as photographs in his room.) She uses the phone's location tracking to lure him to the shore, and while he is following the trail, she digs into the sand on the incoming tide and takes her own life. The film ends with Hae-jun arriving too late at a sea that has filled in, sensing what she has done, and searching for her in desperation.

## Afterwards

It's just an affair movie... and it's made so well..... When it came out I heard so much praise that I never expected it to be *this* much of an affair movie, and yet it should feel unpleasant and it doesn't.... lol A strange film....

Their love is something I can't endorse on my own terms, so for a more comfortable watch I switched my brain off about that part, and then it didn't bother me and I simply enjoyed it. Although there is one point in the middle, on a date, where Hae-jun rubs hand cream into Seo-rae's hands, and that was a bit much.. sir, what are you doing with a wife at home~~~

The material isn't refined, but I could watch the whole thing feeling like it was refined and clean. I can't understand Hae-jun, who lived his whole life as a model detective and then, falling for Seo-rae, threw away not just his fidelity but his pride as a detective — and yet I could understand it because it's Tang Wei. And I can't understand Seo-rae, who kills the husbands who are bad for her while clinging to Hae-jun to the very end — and yet I could understand it because of the situation she's in. That sounds contradictory but it's genuinely how it was. The leads' lines were good too.

I could see exactly why "finally." was a meme for a while — Seo-rae saying it kept circling in my ears long after the film ended.

The scene that stayed with me most was Seo-rae helping Hae-jun, who sleeps badly, get to sleep.

I am (you are?) a jellyfish.

Match your breathing to mine.

She says roughly those lines and helps him sleep as if hypnotising him into being a jellyfish drifting in water, and bringing up jellyfish out of nowhere there was somehow adorable — and since I don't sleep well either, a scene of someone helping you fall asleep beside you left an impression.

So afterwards I tried thinking about jellyfish a few times when I couldn't sleep, and mostly failed... lol I'd be going "I am a jellyfish..." and then a turtle would come chasing me to eat me and I'd go "hmm, jellyfish isn't going to work" and switch to another fish... and try various other fish.. and then thinking about fish reminded me of *Why Fish Don't Exist* and my head filled up again.... There's no helping an N ㅜ
