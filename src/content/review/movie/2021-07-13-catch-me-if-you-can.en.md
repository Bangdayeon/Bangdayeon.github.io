It's genuinely wild that this is based on a true story. Perfect for switching your brain off.

It moves fast, there's nothing that makes you want to tear your hair out, and it has a happy ending.

The lead spends the film hopping from country to country conning people with a smooth tongue and total shamelessness, and maybe because of that the whole mood is cheerful.

## Plot

Frank (DiCaprio), gifted at fraud from birth — impersonating a teacher, forging early-leave notes — runs away from home when he hears his parents are divorcing, then cycles through fake identities and forged cheques until FBI agent Carl (Tom Hanks) starts hunting him.

The two of them go back and forth until Frank is finally caught and locked up, but with Carl's help he ends up working at the FBI spotting forged cheques, closing the book on the criminal life and living as an ace. The bit that stuck with me was Carl asking how he passed the bar exam in — a week? — and Frank answering that he actually just studied for it. Sharp *and* smart.

A genuinely well-made film that also happens to be easy to watch, which I loved.
