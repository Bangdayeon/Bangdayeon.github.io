I don't really watch action, and I've only seen a handful of Marvel films...

Watched it on a whim as a bit of entertainment and had fun.

Stories about enduring a hard childhood and growing into a magnificent adult always frustrate me. (see: Elsa..)

Happiness as a child and happiness as an adult differ in both the process and the destination, and more than anything it kills me that the memories and the time that can only be built *then* are thrown away. Especially when the cause of that hardship is an adult's greed or bad decision.

Still, the action was satisfying and the sister was so cute.

\+ Someone used the phrase "all style, no substance" in the subtitles and it threw me...

Sister: What is it, all style and no substance?

Older sister: No...

"All style, no substance" my foot..

\+ The firefly forest early on was gorgeous.
