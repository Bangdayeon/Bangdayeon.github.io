From the very start you could tell why the IMAX screenings were all sold out..

Timothée's face is luminous and the film is monumental.

The source novel is a sprawling epic, so this feels like just the setup of setup-development-crisis-climax-resolution?

And half of it is Timothée's dreams (the future), so it's mostly seeding — and yet I wasn't sleepy for a second and had a great time.

Timothée's mother's order can control people with their voice ("kneel!" and you kneel..) and that power is so cool. I want it too.

The sandworms are magnificent and Timothée's father is magnificent. My one small regret was wishing they'd hit the blob of a king with the poison while his shield was down.. I hate that blob emperor so much...
