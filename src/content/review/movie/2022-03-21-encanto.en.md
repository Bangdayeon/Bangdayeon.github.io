Wow, this really really stresses me out.
I fell for every single character in Encanto and it stresses me out that this is all there is.

Encanto is short on delicate unpacking of each character and their story. But there are too many characters for that kind of unpacking, and it was never the film's purpose anyway.
Mirabel, who went through failure as a child (not really) and carries the trauma and is still so warm and has no powers (she does, really); Abuela Madrigal, who lost everything when she was young and protected her family and the town with no comfort and no help; Agustín the dad who gets stung by bees constantly; Julieta who heals people by baking; Isabela who grew up as the demure, pretty, elegant woman blooming flowers her whole life and ends up making cacti, vines, sundews and colour-bursting plants; Luisa who looks so sturdy and dependable that she can't show anything fragile; Tía Pepa who can't regulate her mood and is always rained on; Félix beside her, caring for her with his bright good nature; Dolores with her sharp ears and round little cuteness; Camilo with his great personality and humour; Antonio who loves animals and Mirabel; and Bruno, hated and hidden away simply for telling people the future...

If I think about each of them deeply I just cry!!!!

Luisa carrying the rocks, carrying them endlessly, telling everyone to hand it all to her — is that not insane?? Isabela about to go through a marriage she doesn't want for the family's sake, insane?? What about her smiling and flicking flowers at Mirabel? Her way of getting angry being to hit someone with a flower bud ㅜㅜ Dye my hair with pollen forever ㅜㅜㅜ Dolores's eyes and her neat little mouth and nose, and the fact that she alone knew Uncle Bruno was there — so cool. And how much pressure and pain Abuela must have carried with such a young heart, and Antonio simply following Mirabel — also insane.

Maybe I lost my head over the songs and the visuals. But what am I supposed to do when their voices melt me! Dolores and Pepa and Camilo and Antonio and Bruno Madrigal, please sing too ㅜㅠㅜㅜ The dubbed songs are wonderful as well. Isabela's narration line lands like my eardrums are melting. The "iriwak iriwak" bit is slightly too much, but Bruno-no-no's harmony is incredible and Pepa and Félix and Dolores dancing is just unhinged. I think I need to watch the dub too. And at the end there's the scene where Mirabel sings about building the house, and the lyrics go

"Look at this family, a glowing constellation / So full of stars, and everybody wants to shine"

"But the stars don't shine, they burn / And the constellations shift"

and honestly... Mirabel, who couldn't even be in the family photo, standing in the collapsed house with the magic gone, lifting up a family who have lost their magic, telling them: you are stars, and you want to shine, but stars don't shine — they burn, and they move. Telling a family who thought the magic was everything that it isn't about shining but burning, that their existence alone is the miracle, that *that* is the gift and the miracle they kept talking about — it's so.. cool... The Korean version is unhinged too....

Ah, I should have seen it in a theatre ㅜㅠ It looked boring when it came out so I skipped it, but from now on I'm going to see everything that comes out..... I'd love a re-release but it flopped a bit so there's no chance. Ugh ㅜㅠ I wish they'd make Tinker Bell-style shorts for each of them....
