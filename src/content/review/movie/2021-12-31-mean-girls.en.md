A teen movie that's light and fun to watch.

A good teen movie puts me in a good mood. Even the villain is cute, so the whole film is buoyant.

It follows the route where an innocent transfer student becomes the queen bee, behaves badly, and then repents.

Late in the film she comes round, tells her teacher what she did wrong, is forgiven, and goes to the mathletes competition — and there's something she says to herself:

> [!quote]
> Calling somebody else stupid doesn't make you any smarter
> Calling somebody else ugly doesn't make you any prettier
