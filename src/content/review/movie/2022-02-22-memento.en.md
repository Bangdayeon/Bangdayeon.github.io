Spoilers ahead

Nolan is a genius. He runs the present-day thread from the ending backwards to the start, and the past thread from the start forwards to the ending, alternating between them.

So he shows you the ending first and *still* builds a twist, pulling the audience inside the film. Incredible...

Because of that I was genuinely lost at first, but from around the midpoint I was going "this is insane..." and having a great time.

The lead is heartbreaking. Even when he succeeds at his revenge he forgets it straight away, and his last memory resets to the day his wife was dying. Getting the tattoo after succeeding but holding no memory of the revenge, having to live his whole life with the days he believed his wife had died as his final memory — that's sad.

> [!quote] The whole world doesn't just disappear when you close your eyes

That line fits him far too well.
