I honestly just picked a random film to fill in a bingo square with my brain switched off, and it was much more fun than expected.

Maybe because it's the sequel, Cinderella shows a more modern image? here than in the first. She's active!

What Cinderella actually does in the first film: says she wants to see the prince while cleaning, gets a dress thanks to the mice,
has it torn up, goes to the castle thanks to the fairy, dances, runs at midnight,
gets locked in her room by her stepmother when the man with the shoe arrives, cries there until a mouse unlocks the door, then gets escorted to the castle and married, and the story ends.

Laid out like that, Cinderella really doesn't do anything. The mice seem like the protagonists.

## Plot
The second film is three stories in anthology form.

1. Cinderella and the prince plan a party the moment they're back from their honeymoon

She worked nonstop at home and now they've got her working at the palace too. Give it a rest... Anyway, Cinderella can't adapt to palace protocol, goes "I guess I'm not fit to be a princess!", and cries to the mice about her lot — and one of them hands her a handkerchief, which she perches primly on one fingertip to dab her eyes. So funny.. Then, comforted, she plans the party her own way while the man in charge trails after her spamming "this is not according to protocol!!" in agony, which is also funny ㅜㅠ Anyway she does it her way.. sort of commoner-style? and the party is a success.

2. Jaq (the mouse) wants to be useful

Jaq is sad that everything in the palace is too big and he's no longer any help to Cinderelly, so the fairy turns him into a human. It ends with him being useful *as a mouse* and being happy that he is one.

3. Anastasia's love story

Anastasia, who was just a villain in the first film, is the lead. Living an unremarkable life with her mother and sister in the house Cinderella left, she and some baker fall for each other. Anastasia is so cute.. it's a story about her flirting with the baker. Cinderella helps a huge amount. Pulling sly faces and going "mmm~~" while helping lol. What a generous soul.
