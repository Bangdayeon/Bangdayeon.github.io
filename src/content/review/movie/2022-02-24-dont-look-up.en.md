A satire that doesn't hold back. Black comedy!

## Plot
Jennifer discovers a comet predicted to hit in 3 months? 6 months? DiCaprio is a professor and Jennifer is maybe.. a research assistant..? a grad student...? So the two of them take the data to the US presidential candidate, or was it the president, who won't produce a solution right away because the election matters more lol. Anyway, after that they run themselves ragged trying to warn people about it and fix it.

Leo and Jennifer even go on some interview show. The entertainment kind that lots of people watch. Like a celebrity dating gossip programme? Or a Jaejae YouTube episode? Of all the places, they go on *that*. Leo, skilled at handling people, softens it into "everyone, this is dangerous..hehe", while Jennifer, absolutely furious at people not taking them seriously, snaps "do you all want to die?!" — and nobody listens seriously, and Leo tries to calm her down lol

So Leo, articulate and handsome, is liked by everyone, while Jennifer gets branded a weirdo and buried in hate comments...

Leo even has an affair with the interviewer.. and then his wife forgives him, saying he's trash but she cheated when they were dating too. So funny..

And then some doctor confidently announces that he'll destroy the comet with new technology, so they decide to hold that as the last resort. By the time the comet was extremely close...

So the ending —

Earth genuinely gets hit and ends. The professor's group sit around a table having a warm dinner at the end and die in the impact, while the people with money board a ship and leave for another planet lolololol. But the moment they land, the doctor or the president.. gets immediately eaten by some dinosaur-looking creatures. And there was a mummy's-boy presidential son who survives alone in some bunker, and that's the end.

It's funny that Earth actually ends lol. The apocalypse ending...
