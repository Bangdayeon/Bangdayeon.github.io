Watching a famous Korean film is always a strange experience.

First it's strange to see, in the actual film, the celebrated scenes I'd only ever heard about online,

and it's strange to see actors I only knew as memes doing that kind of serious acting.

## Plot

Apparently it's built on the real "war on crime" declared under President Roh Tae-woo. In Busan, where drugs and gambling run rampant, Choi Min-sik — a police officer or a prosecutor — finds some drugs, becomes corrupt, joins hands with Ha Jung-woo's crew and steps into the criminal world.

## Afterwards

I enjoyed it! Partway through there's a whole betrayal-of-a-betrayal-of-a-betrayal thread.

Oh, and Ma Dong-seok appears as Choi Min-sik's daughter's husband? — that kind of role — doing gangster work alongside his father-in-law, and he was so small and thin.

Maybe that's why he was cast in the role of threatening someone and then getting hit ㅜ Or maybe it was meant to convey that the husband was never the sort to do gangster work.

His hairstyle looks like a chestnut burr too

Honestly, Ha Jung-woo might genuinely have trusted Choi Min-sik.
