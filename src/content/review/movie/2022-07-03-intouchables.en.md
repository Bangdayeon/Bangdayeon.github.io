## Plot

The leads are a penniless man who comes to an interview only to get a rejection slip so he can claim state benefits, and a wealthy aristocrat. I couldn't remember either of their names so I've gone with what's on the poster.

The penniless man naturally assumes he'll be rejected and gives a shambolic interview, but the aristocrat likes that he doesn't treat him like a disabled person, drops all the other strong candidates, and hires him as his carer.

Living in that lovely house, he has his conflicts with the aristocrat and helps him too, and grows into a good friend as well as a good carer. He pushes the aristocrat toward his pen pal, pushes the senior carer and the gardener together, gives the ex-boyfriend who dumped the aristocrat's daughter a piece of his mind, and so on — that's how they become friends.

## Afterwards

I enjoyed it. It's an upbeat, warm film.

\+ The penniless man comes on strongly to the aristocrat's secretary, but she has a girlfriend. Did he end up with a partner at the end.. I don't remember him getting one. The aristocrat, the secretary and the senior carer all end up in relationships, and only he just makes lots of friends.
