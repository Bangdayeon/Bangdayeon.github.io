## Plot with spoilers

Olive, the lead, is somewhere between ordinary and nerd, until one day her gossip-loving best friend badgers her so relentlessly that she lies about sleeping with an older guy over the weekend. Except some awful classmates overhear and it spreads across the whole school.. lol

So Olive gets a reputation as "easy", and starts committing the outrage of letting boys who want the credit of having done something with a girl pay her, then allowing them to spread the rumour that they kissed even though they didn't.

She even takes the blame for giving someone an STI!!! Why won't she explain herself!!!!!!!!!! A boy slept with the school nurse? counsellor? and caught an STI, and because that counsellor is the wife of another lovely teacher, Olive feels sorry for the situation and just lets people say she gave it to him — even after the counsellor treated her badly. You absolute fool~~^^

Anyway, she goes around in outfits designed to look even "easier"(?), spreads more rumours, and now gets abuse too. Being born-to-be a nerd, though, she does feel out of place in all this. Then a popular, handsome, tall boy who doesn't care about the rumours says he likes her, and she finally decides to clear the whole thing up.

But that explanation isn't "I never slept with an older guy, boys actually paid me to let them spread rumours about kissing that never happened, and the person who gave him the STI is the counsellor, not me" — it's just vague and roundabout, along the lines of "but now I'm going to find my own happiness", before she goes "oh, I should get going!" and heads off with the popular boy who is now her boyfriend, and the film ends. Ah, a million frustrations 🍠🍠🍠🍠🍠

## Afterwards

I'd watched Decision to Leave on the plane and thought I'd try a refreshing teen film — and it wasn't refreshing at all, it was actually more unpleasant than Decision to Leave lolololol The only fun thing in the film was Emma Stone's face ㅜ so annoying ㅜㅜㅠ

Frustration 1: she could simply have said no, firmly — so why shout that she slept with an older guy?
Frustration 2: why call herself easy and go around performing being easy?
Frustration 3: why cover for the counsellor who never properly counselled her, only sneered at her, who has an STI and slept with a student while married?
Frustration 4: she says she'll explain and then doesn't explain??????

Olive genuinely did nothing and got torn apart at school over rumours, right up until the end....

I'm not saying Olive did well, mind... attention-seeking...

The A on her chest is because she read that old story about a scarlet letter, decided it was exactly her situation, and started wearing it...

Completely lost it
