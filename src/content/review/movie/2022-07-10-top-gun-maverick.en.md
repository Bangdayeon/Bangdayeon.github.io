## Plot

Maverick, a Top Gun graduate from decades ago, does whatever he likes.

However much his superiors tell him to stop, he flies expensive jets as he pleases, pushes too far, and ignores orders.

So Maverick, nearly fired, becomes an instructor for the Top Gun trainees. He's there to train them for a mission to destroy a dangerous nuclear plant.

Back in the town near Top Gun after a long absence, he hits on his ex-girlfriend? and gets thrown out of a bar by the hot-blooded trainees, that sort of thing.

Maverick really can fly, and he says that to get everyone through the mission alive he has to break his superior's rules again and train them his own way, so he runs dangerous lessons.

Along the way he clashes with Rooster, the son of the friend who died years ago, but they make up properly, keep training, and go on the mission.

## Afterwards

I'd meant to watch the original Top Gun before Maverick, but I kept putting it off and just went without it.

And it was genuinely more fun than I expected.

The mood of Rooster playing the piano and singing was lovely, and — what was it — anyway, the scene with the trainees playing ball on the beach had a great mood too, and above all the flight scenes are so well shot. I genuinely felt like I was in the jet.

The mission was so hard — flying through low, winding mountains at speed to dodge automatic lasers that finish you in one hit, blowing open a hole the size of an ant's backside, dropping the bomb in, pulling up hard to escape, dodging every laser and getting home (plus if they aren't quick they'll be spotted by enemies with the newest fighters, while the Top Gun crew fly clapped-out jets..) — that having grown attached to all of them I was sure at least one would die and watched with my heart in my throat.

Thankfully nobody dies, and at the end the stranded Maverick and Rooster smashing two? of the newest fighters in a junk plane, and the perpetually smug Hangman turning up to chase down the last one, was very cool.

Rooster had wanted to be Top Gun, but because of his mother's dying wish Maverick kept failing him at the academy despite him being more than good enough, so Rooster deeply resented him — and after talking it out and, above all, seeing how extraordinary Maverick's flying is, they reconcile properly.

Maverick hanging out at his ex-girlfriend's house, then escaping through the window when her daughter arrives and getting caught anyway, was funny too.

And it was remarkable how handsome Tom Cruise is..
