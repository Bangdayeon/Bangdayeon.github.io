## Plot

Ah, there's rather a lot going on here..

In the Goryeo era some strange monster is running around hurting people. A dimensional door opens in mid-air, a car comes out, and Guard (Kim Woo-bin) steps out of it and takes the monster away. In the damage a baby is left alone in the house, and the small robot that follows Kim Woo-bin secretly brings the baby (Ean) to the present and simply raises her.

Ean grows up bright and reaches primary school. She's always suspected her dad (Guard), and one day she witnesses proof that he's an alien — she sees them locking alien prisoners inside human bodies.

Then Mun Do-seok (So Ji-sub), who holds the leader prisoner, is made aware of his own identity by another criminal alien and begins a campaign to convert Earth's atmosphere into that of the aliens' planet. To stop them, Guard, the small robot and Ean seal the alien group in the Goryeo era. The three of them try to return to the present, but something goes wrong, they're trapped in Goryeo, and Ean ends up alone.

In the process they lose the divine sword that matters so much to the aliens, and once it's in the Goryeo era it becomes rumoured as a valuable object, so the Taoist Mureuk (Ryu Jun-yeol) and the Yeom Jung-ah couple go hunting for it. That entangles them with the grown-up Ean, who's looking for the sword to get back to the present, and with the aliens, who are also looking for it to get back. The four groups fight it out until Mureuk recovers the hazy childhood memory he'd lost, realises something, and the film ends.

## Afterwards

**Honestly, one and a half stars belong to Kim Tae-ri.** Because I'm a fan. Her keeping the gun she carried as a baby and growing up to fight relying on that one gun is absurd but so cute...

Looking at the poster I genuinely wondered whether to skip it, then went anyway on the grounds that anything with Kim Tae-ri will do. And it was more fun than I expected. The imagination is great, and I thought the villain alien in the middle was really well made.

And the Yeom Jung-ah couple were adorable. They get tricked by the aliens into eating poisoned food, cast a paralysis spell on themselves before it spreads, and then scramble to find and take the antidote — funny. Ryu Jun-yeol turns up midway, swipes the antidote meant for Kim Tae-ri, and casually drops one off for the couple too.

Also, I'd assumed the Yeom Jung-ah couple only carried useless junk, but they have a lot of genuinely good things.

Basically it was great fun every time Kim Tae-ri was on screen. So cute...

Bits I remember not liking:

First, the scene where Guard's arm is torn off... he's on his knees going "aaargh!" while it's ripped away and it made me cringe. The sudden upper-body close-up focusing on his face felt like watching Power Rangers, which annoyed me a bit. He's supposedly a robot and it hurts a lot
![Guard's arm being torn off](_img/2022-07-24-alienoid-01.webp)

And the Taoists leaping far and almost flying when they jump also annoyed me slightly. Or maybe it's that Ryu Jun-yeol's character is slightly annoying. The aliens smash a thatched house once and Ryu Jun-yeol tramples through it twice.. sorry, commoners ㅜㅠ
![The Taoist Ryu Jun-yeol running](_img/2022-07-24-alienoid-02.webp)

The slow-motion shot of Kim Tae-ri sliding on her knees and hitting a moving alien might also have annoyed me if it weren't Kim Tae-ri.
![Kim Tae-ri firing](_img/2022-07-24-alienoid-03.webp)

And the film keeps moving between the present and Goryeo, past and present, and the seams and the ordering and the wrap-up weren't clean. If you switch off even slightly you might lose the thread.

I think I might not have followed it if it had been a foreign film.

Still, I enjoyed it. I definitely saw that it was Part 1 and went in thinking "ah, so there'll be a Part 2..", and then Ryu Jun-yeol remembers at the very end that the alien So Ji-sub was carrying (the strongest alien) had gone into his own body as a child, and the film ended. I was concentrating, and it finishing right at that moment — did the Alienoid Part 2 title card come up? — anyway it ended and I went "oh right, this was Part 1!!!" and felt sad.

Ah, I was enjoying it and now I have to wait!!

Thinking about it ending that way, I can already picture the scenes that'll annoy me. Because to kill an alien you have to kill the human host.

Everyone might grieve over Ryu Jun-yeol having to die and then he survives fine, and Ean says "thanks, everyone" and returns to the present.. or Guard might just die, or come back alive going "well, I'm a robot". Then maybe the mini robot and Guard and Ean all live.. Lee Ha-nee, who chased Guard around calling him handsome, also gets a shot at the very end, so thinking about her being in it makes it sound fun again.

That said, I can see why it flopped. Tickets are expensive and there's a lot worth seeing lately — Top Gun, Emergency Declaration?, Hunt — so I doubt people would pay fifteen thousand won for something this divisive. Cinemas are so expensive these days ㅜ Still, I hope it does well.
