Upside of not being clever: you can enjoy a film all the way to the end
Had fun with it. But it's a bit frustrating.. the cop is bad and the daughter is heartbreaking

The protagonist, who has dementia, was once a serial killer.
Except that, apart from his last killing, he only killed bad people. The problem is that a clean-cut police officer (actually a killer) turns up around this man's daughter, and the two of them detect(?) that the other is a killer.

But because the protagonist has dementia he keeps losing his memory, so he doesn't know whether the threat approaching his daughter is his own doing or the cop's. He keeps forgetting ㅜ

That frustrated me a lot. It must have been far more frustrating for him...

Honestly I just kept wishing neither of them were anywhere near the daughter. She's so lovely and so kind..
