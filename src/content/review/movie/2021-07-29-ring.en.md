Ring showed up in the horror slot of a "back to the roots" series, and I wanted to watch something scary, so I went with the famous one.

## Plot
It opens with a student watching a video tape and dying of a heart attack in a bizarre posture. The protagonist, her relative — a reporter, I think.. — hears talk about the tape around the funeral and goes to the cabin where the student watched it. She finds nothing, until at some shop? lost-and-found? she comes across a suspicious tape and watches it. Sigh ㅜ Then the phone rings, she picks up, and learns she'll die in a week. To stop it she shows a copy to her ex-husband and together they dig into the secret of the woman on the tape. But she doesn't look after the tape properly and her young son ends up watching it too. Sigh........

## Afterwards
It just wasn't scary. Maybe because it's old? It's the "you can only live by passing the tape to someone else, and watching it kills you" kind of story...? There weren't even any real jump scares. Partway through I got to see the famous shot of the ghost crawling out of the TV, which was neat. It was sad that Ring's mascot ghost is a girl who died unjustly.

The protagonist kindly resolves the ghost's grudge, but resolving it doesn't stop the tape's curse. Just that kind of film.. not scary, though it did seem well made. I think I saw that there's a sequel, but I don't especially want to watch it.

I really want to see a properly scary horror film..
