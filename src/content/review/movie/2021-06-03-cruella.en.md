Spoilers ahead

Wow.. shockingly more fun than I expected. The visuals, Emma Stone's face, the clothes, the music, the story — all of it. Even the ending was great.

## The Baroness
The Baroness as the villain was such a good character. She scouts Estella, then rips up a dress Estella made, and when Estella cuts her hand she sees the blood and goes "get me fabric in *this* colour!" — and early on we also see her finish a meal and chuck the rubbish out of the car window. Watching it I thought "wow, so artists really are lunatics.." and it turns out she is genuinely insane.
Later, when Estella brings her a stunning dress, she looks moved and says "I've done it again!!"..

## Cruella and the Baroness
But because both of them are unhinged with no brakes, the whole thing rips along. Styling the mannequin with crumpled newspaper, the rubbish dress, the moth dress, the rock fashion show — there isn't a scene to throw away. Two enormously strong characters and neither gets flattened, and the story keeps moving. Loved it.

## Other thoughts
After the film the cast intro reel plays like a fashion show, and that mood was lovely too.

I saw it with Ju-hee and then again later with Sohyun, and it was still fresh and fun the second time.

What ached a bit: wishing her mother could have been there for the Cruella who finally succeeded on her own terms, and Cruella crying in the fire.

Apart from Cruella's monologues, everything is brisk. At the end, to finish the Baroness off properly, Estella designs her own death at the Baroness's hands in front of a crowd — and pulls it off.

With that, Estella holds her own funeral, kills Estella off, and lives on as Cruella. And it isn't a mask or anything — it genuinely is Estella's true self, and standing at her own grave she coolly says "this is too depressing, put on something upbeat" and walks off. So cool..

Also the way Cruella says "darling, darling" to her friends is devastating. And when Estella dyes her half-and-half hair red for the first time and the film jumps to her grown up, Emma Stone's face appearing out of nowhere is also devastating. I want to see her in person once — how are her eyes and nose and mouth all that big and pretty, I'm so curious.
