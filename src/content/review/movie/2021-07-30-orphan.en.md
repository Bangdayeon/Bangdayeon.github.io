Spoilers ahead

After Ring I posted on my Instagram story that it wasn't scary at all, got horror recommendations (thanks Se-rin), and finally watched Orphan!

Orphan had more bite than Ring, so it was more fun. Maybe because it isn't a ghost story. But it is *maddeningly* frustrating.

Is this a horror thing? Why do the people who won't listen make me angrier than the main villain?

The girl I can accept — right, she was simply born a psychopath. But people like the husband, with that quiet low-grade selfishness, are the ones who wear me down.

At the end the psycho falls into the frozen lake and still hides a knife while going "Mommy, save me!!1", and the mother swears at her and kicks her off. If this had been a franchise it might have ended with them saving the psycho, fighting, and her going to a psychiatric hospital with an escape hinted at — but Orphan's mother trusts her own sweet children and, without a moment's hesitation, curses the psycho out and finishes it. Very satisfying~
