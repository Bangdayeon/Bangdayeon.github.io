Ah, the elephant voiced by Tori Kelly is so cute. Elephants really are so cute, so so cute. Meena is enormous and still so cute.

The others are all so cute too. Cute animals getting through hardship and trials to reach the biggest stage — there was no way I wasn't watching this.

2 was as good as 1. If 1 had the incredible songs, 2 — being about the biggest stage — has insane visuals. It genuinely felt like watching a musical. All of them had their own trauma, and I loved that they overcame it and pulled the show off beautifully.

Honestly I don't remember the songs in 2 all that clearly. But the visuals were *so* good! The mother pig conquering her fear of heights and the trash wolf flying successfully while saving the koala being thrown, the gorilla's staff fight, the wolf's daughter running between jellies while singing, Meena performing — all of it was magnificent.
