Spoilers ahead

Cinderella 2 and 3 are pretty obscure so I assumed they'd be dull, but I had a great time all the way through 3 as well.

The third one seems a bit more deranged. For a start Cinderella's face going off-model is funny, and the prince is somehow strange too.

## Plot
At the anniversary festival, Anastasia picks up the fairy's wand. Wait, I thought Ana was living happily with the baker in 2, why has it gone back to this ㅜ no idea ㅜ but with that wand the stepmother rewinds time to the day of the glass slipper to get revenge on Cinderelly lol. She tampers with the prince's memory and everything..

One of the funniest bits: partway through the prince somehow recovers his memory and goes to find the real Cinderelly, and the king, in pink underpants, goes "you shall not take one step down these stairs!" — so the prince bungees out of the window next to him.. leaping in a pose straight out of some weird meme.

Anastasia spends the whole film going true love, true love, chasing true love, and ends up receiving the king's true-love token. Some sort of pinecone-looking thing.. or was it a ring..? It was his late wife's keepsake that he'd set aside for his daughter-in-law, and he gives it to Anastasia even knowing she isn't the real one. Kind king...
