## Plot

Two Black FBI agents are assigned to escort two rich daughters, and on the drive from the airport they have a car accident that leaves the girls with minor facial injuries. The girls make a huge fuss about not being able to go to the party looking like this, so the two agents disguise themselves as them and go instead.

Once there, all kinds of things happen and it's funny — driving around with the girls' friends listening to hip-hop, verbally destroying a rival, eating cheese despite a cheese allergy and having to go to the toilet, shopping for clothes that don't fit at all, getting hit on by someone. At the end the girls' friends even say the agents were more fun than the girls and ask to hang out again.

The "mama time~~ it's mama time~~~" bit is unhinged and the head-spinning in the club is hilarious lol

Also, the original girls' friends going "oh, it's my friends~~" and hanging out with people who obviously have a completely different build, face and personality is funny in itself.

Shopping with two grown men, going "hmm~~.. no, you can totally wear it!!" — they seem so good-natured too.

Just a fun, light watch.
