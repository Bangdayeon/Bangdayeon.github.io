Finally watched this famous one. It is so~~~~ frustrating. No, suffocating.

All I knew was that it's based on the Hwaseong serial murders. I didn't know the details, and it turns out to be about investigators failing to catch the culprit using investigation methods of that era that barely qualify as investigation.

The evidence is always just out of reach. They try to force whichever suspect they have into being the culprit, and while they do, more victims keep appearing.

At the end comes "Do you eat breakfast?", delivered with Song Kang-ho's face filling the frame and his eyes fixed straight down the camera. Bong Joon-ho said it was what he wanted to say to the culprit, who hadn't been caught when he made it. I think the title is well chosen. It's a film addressed to the culprit, so it includes words spoken to him, and the title makes it a memory from the culprit's point of view.

The culprit was identified in 2019. He'd seen the film too, and reportedly felt nothing much about it. Really.. what is to be done about people like that...? Maybe something was wrong from birth. Could he have become a better person raised with the right education and environment? But victims already exist because of him and it can't be undone, so I'm not sure what the point of asking is.

[https://www.donga.com/news/Society/article/all/20201103/103764334/1](https://www.donga.com/news/Society/article/all/20201103/103764334/1)

Memories of Murder is frustrating and infuriating both as a film and as the reality it came from.
