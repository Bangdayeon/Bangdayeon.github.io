## Plot

Hiccup, far too weak to be a Viking, one day happens to hit and bring down a dragon said to be enormously powerful using a weapon he built himself — and then can't kill the injured dragon and helps it instead. Observing the dragon closely, he learns that real dragons are different from what people had believed. Dragons aren't terrifying monsters at all, they're basically big cats!

So Hiccup becomes friends with Toothless, the dragon he found, and because Toothless's tail was disabled because of him, he builds a prosthetic fin so they can fly together. Then they happen into the dragons' nest, and he learns that the reason dragons had been stealing the Vikings' food was that a giant dragon was making them do it.

He tells his father, the Vikings' chief, but his father won't listen, captures Toothless, uses him as a compass and goes to the nest to exterminate the dragons. They smash the nest and wake the giant dragon, and of course the Vikings are all flattened... At which point Hiccup and his friends join forces with the dragons to beat the giant one, and the dragons are freed from being errand-runners.

Hiccup loses a leg in the fight, so now he and Toothless have matching legs, and dragons and Vikings stop fighting, cooperate and live happily.

## Afterwards

It's remarkable that I hadn't seen this until now... lol I watched Easy A on the plane, felt gross about it, needed a proven good film, and picked this!

Toothless just being a cat is so cute, and it was so sad that the dragons were errand-runners fetching food for the giant dragon at home and then, if caught by Vikings, became fight-training fodder in the Viking village — so I'm so glad they're finally free of both ㅜㅜ

Hiccup losing a leg at the end was a slight shock, but he's a bit surprised and then, maybe because most Vikings are missing some part of themselves, doesn't seem too shaken — so my shock disappeared along with his.

Anyway it's just good... cute and fun and healing and wonderful....
